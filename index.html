Questions

Is Velyn a medical device or contraception?

No. Velyn gives tracking and general health information from official sources. It does not diagnose and must not be used as contraception.

Where is my data?

On your phone, in an encrypted database. We do not have a copy. You can export or delete it at any time.

What happens if I change phones?

Create a backup file protected by your own password, then restore it on the new phone.

Which languages?

17 languages, including English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese and Dutch.

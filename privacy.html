Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Velyn is a period, fertility, pregnancy and menopause tracker. This policy explains, in plain words, what happens to your information. The short version: your health data stays on your phone, encrypted. We do not sell it, share it with advertisers, or put tracking tools in the app.

1. Who we are

Velyn ("we") is the developer of the Velyn app. Contact: CONTACT_EMAIL.

2. What the app stores, and where

InformationWhere it is keptDo we receive it?
Your goal (cycle, trying to conceive, pregnancy, postpartum, perimenopause), birth year, period days, flow, symptoms, notes, settingsOnly on your phone, in a database encrypted with a key kept in your phone's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain)No
App PIN and the fingerprint/face settingYour phone's secure storage. Fingerprint and face checks are done by your phone's system; the app never sees themNo
RemindersScheduled by your phone. Discreet wording by defaultNo

The app works fully without an account and without an internet connection.

3. When information leaves your phone (only if you choose)

  • Backup file: when you create a backup, the app encrypts it with a password you choose (AES-GCM) and lets you save it where you want. We cannot open it. If you lose the password, nobody can recover it.
  • Spreadsheet export (CSV): a plain, unencrypted copy you choose to share. The app warns you before.
  • Doctor report (PDF): created on your phone and shared only with whom you choose.
  • Partner sharing: you choose what is shared and send it yourself. Notes are never shared.

3b. Health article updates

Once a day the app downloads the latest version of its official health articles from our website (kamalzarouala.github.io). This download sends no information about you; like any web request, GitHub (which hosts the site) sees your IP address.

4. Article translation (optional)

Health library articles are in English. If you choose "Translate into my language", the translation is done on your phone by Google ML Kit. The article text is not sent anywhere. The first time, the app downloads a language file from Google. Google may collect basic usage and performance data about the translation tool (not your health data and not the article text). See ML Kit terms. If you never tap "Translate", this does not happen.

4b. Purchases (Velyn Plus)

Payments are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We use RevenueCat, a subscription service, to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID, your purchase receipt and basic device/store information. It does not receive your health data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. We never see your card details.

5. What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising or analytics SDKs (no Facebook, TikTok, AppsFlyer, Google Analytics).
  • No selling or renting of data. No sharing of health data with anyone.
  • No analysis of photos. The discharge reference pictures are built into the app; you do not upload photos.
  • No screenshot detection.

6. Health information

Health information in the app comes from official public sources (for example the NHS) and is shown with its source. Velyn does not diagnose and is not a medical device. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

7. Your rights

Because your data is on your phone, you control it: you can view, export, or delete it at any time. Uninstalling the app deletes the app's data from your phone (backup files you saved elsewhere are not affected). If you live in the EU/UK (GDPR), California (CCPA/CPRA), Washington State (My Health My Data Act) or elsewhere, you also have the right to ask us what personal data we hold about you; for health data, the answer is: none on our servers. For purchase records, contact us at the address above.

8. Children

Velyn is not intended for children under 13. Teenagers should use it with a parent or guardian's knowledge where local law requires it.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app before they apply. We will never move a free protection feature behind payment or start selling data.

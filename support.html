Common questions

I changed phone. How do I keep my data?
In the app: Me → Backup → create a backup file with a password, then restore it on the new phone.

How do I cancel Velyn Plus?
Google Play → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions → Velyn → Cancel. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

How do I delete everything?
Uninstall the app. Your data lives only on your phone, so it is deleted with the app.
